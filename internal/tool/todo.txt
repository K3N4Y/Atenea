Maintain a live checklist for non-trivial multi-step work. Use it for roughly three or more steps, or whenever the user requests a task list; omit it for simple one-step work.

## Input grammar
- `todos` is the complete replacement list, not a partial update.
- Every item has short imperative `content` and a `status` of `pending`, `in_progress`, or `completed`.
- Keep exactly one item `in_progress` while work remains.

## Examples
- `{"todos":[{"content":"Reproduce the bug","status":"in_progress"},{"content":"Implement the fix","status":"pending"},{"content":"Run focused tests","status":"pending"}]}` starts a three-step task.
- `{"todos":[{"content":"Reproduce the bug","status":"completed"},{"content":"Implement the fix","status":"in_progress"},{"content":"Run focused tests","status":"pending"}]}` advances the checklist.

## Recoverable failures
- Invalid status: replace it with one of the three supported values.
- Missing item content: provide a short imperative action.
- Multiple items in progress: submit the full list again with exactly one current item.
- Stale checklist: replace the entire list with current state.

## Anti-patterns
- WRONG: send only the changed item. RIGHT: send the complete list every time.
- WRONG: use sentence-length progress narration as item content. RIGHT: use a short imperative action.
- WRONG: stop working after updating the list. RIGHT: continue with the active task.

<critical>
- Every call REPLACES the entire checklist.
- Keep exactly one item `in_progress` until all work is complete.
- Mark completed work promptly and advance the next item in the same or following call.
</critical>
